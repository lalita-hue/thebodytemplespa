Before you come in

Two things that make a real difference to your result.

Let it grow two to three weeks Wax needs about a quarter inch of hair to grip, on the face and on the body. Resist tweezing or shaving in the week before — on brows those strays are exactly what we need in order to see and correct your shape.
Pause retinol and acids for 5–7 days On any area being waxed. Retinol, tretinoin, glycolic and salicylic acids thin the surface of the skin, and waxing over them can lift skin rather than hair. Tell us at booking if you have taken Accutane in the last year.
