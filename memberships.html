Questions

Good to know.

How does the membership work?

You pay a one-time $50 setup fee, then choose one membership: Rejuvenate, Deep Recovery, Lash Lift or Lymphatic 100. Each month you receive that service at the member rate.

What is the $50 setup fee?

It's a one-time fee you pay when you join. It applies to every membership and is not charged again each month.

What if I can't come in one month?

Your session is never wasted. You can gift that month's service to anyone you like — a friend, partner or family member — or let it roll over to the next month.

How long is the commitment?

You choose: a 6-month or a 12-month commitment. Pick the one that fits your goals when you join.

What does the 10% discount cover?

As a member you get 10% off all of our services: massage, lymphatic drainage, facials, acne treatments, peels, lashes and brows, waxing, permanent makeup, body contouring, reflexology and functional longevity health coaching. Events, retreats and certifications aren't part of the 10%, but members get special member pricing on them.

Which membership is right for me?

Choose Rejuvenate for stress and everyday aches, Deep Recovery for tight muscles and active bodies, Lash Lift for effortless lashes, or Lymphatic 100 for swelling, bloating and post-op recovery. Not sure? Text us and we'll help you pick.

How do I sign up?

Tap "Join" on the membership you want, or call or text us at (959) 400-9242 and we'll get you set up.
