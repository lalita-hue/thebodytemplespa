Our lash artist will reach out shortly to talk through how your lashes behave and whether a lift, a tint, or both is the right call.

The Korean method lifts from the root rather than curling the whole lash into a hook, so it holds on stubborn, straight lashes that other techniques give up on — and a keratin step goes on while the lash is still open, so your own lashes grow back fuller and stronger.

Beauty starts with health. Non-toxic products, a clean unrushed room, and processing timed to your own lash thickness rather than a standard clock — which is the whole difference between a soft curl and a kinked one.
