# The Ascension Room — Rodrigo Serrudo (Virtus Artifex)

General-public version: he trains athletes, this conversation is for
everyone else. Full question outline lives with the host; this file keeps
the opening and the open items.

## Host opening (~60 seconds)

> Most of us never notice the day we stopped being strong.
>
> There's no injury. No single moment. Just a slow drift — the stairs feel
> a little longer, the groceries a little heavier, getting up off the floor
> takes a hand on the couch. We call it getting older. We call it being
> tired. And years later we realize something is gone.
>
> Welcome to The Ascension Room.
>
> My guest today is Rodrigo Serrudo, strength coach and founder of Virtus
> Artifex. In Latin, the craftsman of strength — and *virtus* means more
> than muscle. It means courage. Character. The excellence of the whole
> person.
>
> Rodrigo spends his days building athletes. But this conversation isn't
> for athletes. It's for the 45-year-old at a desk, for the mother who
> wants to keep up with her grandchildren, for anyone who has quietly
> started to feel their body becoming unreliable.
>
> Because if the body is a temple, strength might be the part of its care
> we've forgotten most.
>
> Rodrigo, welcome.
