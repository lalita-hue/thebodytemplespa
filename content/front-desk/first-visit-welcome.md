# First-Visit Welcome: Steps & Script

For any team member greeting a **first-time client** at The Body Temple Spa (Prince Ave, Athens, GA).
Goal: the client feels expected, cared for and safe, learns what makes us different (our water, our longevity
approach), confirms their intake form, and leaves knowing what else we can do for them, without feeling sold to.

> **To confirm with Lalita:** the water's brand/system name, whether clients can buy water or a system to take home,
> whether the neck wrap is warmed (and with what, e.g. lavender), and any first-visit offer for rebooking.

---

| # | Step | Time | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|

| 1 | Greet at the door | 0:00 | Greeted by name within 10 seconds |

Total before the session: **about 8–10 minutes.** Build it into the first appointment.

---

## Step 0: Prepare before they arrive
- Open their appointment and **intake form** in GHL. Note anything to ask about (missing answers, medications,
  pregnancy, recent surgery or procedures, allergies, skin conditions, goals).
- Know their **first name** and **service** before they walk in.
- Start warming the **neck wrap**.
- Water station stocked and glasses ready; kettle hot for tea.
- Lobby tidy, music soft, lighting calm.

## Step 1: Greet at the door
Stand up, smile, make eye contact, use their name.

> "Hi, you must be **[Name]**! Welcome to The Body Temple Spa. We've been expecting you."

## Step 2: Welcome speech + offer a seat
Walk them to the seat. Speak slowly and warmly.

> "Please, have a seat and make yourself comfortable. This is your time.
>
> Since this is your first visit, I want to welcome you properly. The Body Temple Spa is Athens' first longevity spa.
> Everything we do, from the products we use to the water we serve, is chosen to support your health, not just
> how you look today. We believe beauty starts with health.
>
> So for the next few minutes, all you need to do is breathe and relax. We'll take care of the rest."

## Step 3: Offer our water (or tea)
Hand them the glass, don't just offer it.

> "Before we start, I'd love to offer you a glass of our water. We serve **hydrogen alkaline, medical-grade water**
> here. It's one of the things our clients love most about visiting us. Hydration matters for everything we do,
> especially for your skin and for lymphatic work, so we like to start every visit this way.
>
> Or, if you'd prefer, we have **[tea options]**. What sounds good to you?"

*If they ask more about the water:*
> "It's filtered, alkaline water infused with hydrogen. Many people find it feels softer and easier to drink,
> which helps them drink more. We drink it all day here. [If we sell it: We can tell you more about having it at home
> after your session.]"

## Step 4: Review the intake form together
Sit beside them (not across a desk). Keep the tone caring, not clinical.

> "I've looked over your intake form, thank you for filling it out. I'd like to go over a few things with you,
> just to make sure your treatment today is completely safe and made for you."

Check, in this order:
1. **Goals:** "What's the main thing you'd love to feel or see from today?"
2. **Health changes:** "Has anything changed since you filled this out?"
3. **Medications & supplements:** "Are you taking anything new?"
4. **Pregnancy / nursing** (when relevant to the service).
5. **Recent surgery, procedures, injections or skin treatments** (post-op clients: date of surgery, surgeon's clearance).
6. **Allergies & sensitivities** (products, latex, fragrance, foods for wellness clients).
7. **Missing answers:** fill them in together.
8. **Signature** on consent.

> "Perfect, thank you. Is there anything else you'd like us to know before we begin, anything at all?"

If something is a concern for the service, **do not guess**. Let the practitioner know before the session starts.

## Step 5: Place the neck wrap
Ask permission first.

> "I'm going to place a **warm neck wrap** on your shoulders while you finish your water. It helps your body let go
> of tension and start relaxing before your treatment. May I?"
>
> "How does that feel? Too warm?"

## Step 6: Share our services (tailored to their goals)
The client is relaxed and seated, so this is the moment. Keep it to about a minute. Lead with what **matches their goals
from the intake form**, then briefly mention the rest. Hand them the **service menu card**.

> "While you relax, I'd love to give you a quick picture of everything we offer, so you know we're here for you
> whenever you need us."

**Our services (say it naturally, not as a list):**
- **Lymphatic drainage:** wellness, post-op and lymphedema, targeted or full-body, with session packages.
  *Add-on:* our **Vibration Plate Lymphatic Reset** for $15.
- **Body contouring & sculpting:** non-surgical, no downtime, for the waist, back, legs, arms, jawline and cellulite.
- **Facials & skincare:** advanced skin analysis first, then facials, sculpting, dermaplaning, collagen,
  chemical peels and acne treatments.
- **Korean lash lift & tint.**
- **Permanent makeup:** brows and lips, designed for your face.
- **Waxing:** face and body, with hard wax and non-toxic aftercare.
- **Wellness beyond the spa:** The Feminine Gut community for gut and hormone health, and our hair mineral test,
  The Hair Temple Blueprint, for women who want to stop guessing about what their body needs.

> "Since you mentioned **[their goal]**, I think you'd really love **[1 matching service]**. Your practitioner
> can tell you more after your session today. No pressure at all, I just want you to know it's here for you."

**Goal → service ideas:**
| They said… | Suggest |
|---|---|
| Puffiness, swelling, heaviness, post-surgery | Lymphatic drainage, Vibration Plate add-on |
| Stubborn areas, shape, cellulite | Body contouring & sculpting |
| Breakouts, dullness, fine lines | Skin analysis + facial or peel |
| Bloating, cravings, hormones, fatigue | The Feminine Gut, the hair mineral test |
| Saving time getting ready | Lash lift & tint, permanent makeup brows |

## Step 7: Walk them to the room
> "Your room is ready. **[Practitioner name]** will be taking care of you today. Bring your water with you.
> Your session will take about **[length]**, and afterwards we'll go over how to keep your results going at home.
> Enjoy, this time is all yours."

Introduce the practitioner by name and pass on anything important from the intake review.

## Step 8: After the session (bonus)
- **Refill their water:** "Let's refill your water. Keep drinking today, it helps your body after your treatment."
- **Aftercare:** practitioner explains what to do at home (and sends the aftercare by email/text).
- **Next visit:** "Based on how your session went, **[Practitioner]** recommends coming back in **[timeframe]**.
  Would you like to book that now so you get the time you want?"
- **Review:** "If you loved your visit, a Google review would mean so much to our small team."
- **Goodbye:** "Thank you for trusting us, **[Name]**. Welcome to The Body Temple. We can't wait to see you again."
