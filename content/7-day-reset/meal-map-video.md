## Script

### 1. Hook + welcome (0:00–0:45)

> If you've ever started a new plan on a Monday full of hope, and by Thursday you were standing in the kitchen at 3 PM wondering why you're *starving*… this week is going to feel different.
>
> Welcome to your 7-Day Feminine GLP-1 & Nervous System Reset. I'm Lalita, and this is Week 1: **Nourish First.**
>
> Grab your Meal Map PDF. Print it if you can, and let's walk through it together.

### 2. Reset the rules: this is not a diet (0:45–2:00)

> Before we talk about food, I want to reset the rules.
>
> This is **not** a diet. It's not a cleanse, not a challenge, and not a 30-day fix. There are no forbidden foods and there are no perfect days. You cannot fail this.
>
> This week we are not taking anything away. We're only **adding.** Protein, fiber and water. That's it.
>
> If you miss a meal or a day, that's not a detour, that's part of the journey. You just pick up at the next meal. Progress over perfection. Self-compassion over self-control.

### 3. Why nourish first (2:00–3:15)

> Here's why we start here.
>
> GLP-1 is the hormone your gut makes that tells your brain, "We're satisfied, we're safe, we can stop." It steadies your blood sugar and quiets cravings.
>
> When you under-eat or skip meals, your body goes into conservation mode. It slows your metabolism, turns up your hunger hormones and turns *down* your GLP-1 sensitivity. That's why restriction always backfires. You end up fighting your own biology.
>
> Your body responds to abundance with wisdom, and to scarcity with panic. So this week, every meal sends your body one message: *I'm here to support you, not starve you.*

### 4. Your 3 Daily Wins (3:15–6:00)

> On page 1 you'll see your 3 Daily Wins. These are the only three things I'm asking of you this week.
>
> **Win number one: Protein.** 20 to 30 grams at each meal. Protein is the strongest natural trigger for GLP-1. When it reaches your small intestine, your body releases GLP-1 and you *feel* full, and you stay full.
>
> What does 20 to 30 grams look like?
> - 3 eggs is about 18 grams.
> - 1 cup of Greek yogurt is about 20 grams.
> - 4 ounces of salmon is about 28 grams.
> - 1 cup of cooked lentils is about 18 grams.
> - 1 scoop of protein powder is usually 20 to 25 grams.
>
> The biggest shift: have your protein at **breakfast**, within one hour of waking. Most of us save all our protein for dinner. Toast and coffee in the morning is exactly why you're hungry by 10.
>
> If this is a lot more protein than you're used to, start at 15 to 20 grams and build up over a few days.
>
> **Win number two: Fiber.** A source of soluble fiber at every meal. Fiber is your gut bacteria's favorite food, including Akkermansia. When they ferment that fiber, they make compounds that tell your gut to release more GLP-1.
>
> Think berries, apples with the skin, avocado, oats, ground flax, chia, lentils and beans. And your prebiotic foods: onion, garlic, leeks, asparagus and green banana. These are like fertilizer for your GLP-1 factory.
>
> Go slowly. If you're not used to fiber, add one new food every few days, drink your water, and choose cooked veggies over raw if you get bloated.
>
> **Win number three: Water.** Half your body weight in ounces. So if you weigh 150 pounds, that's about 75 ounces a day. Start with 16 to 20 ounces as soon as you wake up. Try to drink about 30 minutes before meals rather than with them, and ease off 2 to 3 hours before bed so you're not up all night.
>
> A lot of what feels like hunger is actually thirst. And your gut bacteria need water to do their job. If plain water bores you, add lemon, cucumber or mint.

### 5. Walking through the 7-Day Meal Map (6:00–7:45)

> Now look at the table. Seven days, breakfast, lunch, dinner and a snack.
>
> Every meal already has your protein and your fiber built in. You don't have to count anything. Just follow the map.
>
> - **Breakfasts** rotate between three: a Greek yogurt bowl with berries, flax, chia and walnuts; a 3-egg veggie omelet with spinach and avocado; and overnight oats with protein powder, apple and cinnamon.
> - **Lunches** are built from leftovers on purpose: lentil-leek soup, a salmon salad, a quinoa bowl and a chicken salad.
> - **Dinners** rotate between baked salmon with asparagus and sweet potato, a turkey stir-fry, and chicken thighs with Brussels sprouts and cauliflower mash.
> - **Snacks** are there if you're hungry, not because you have to. Apple with almond butter, a kefir smoothie, boiled eggs, hummus and cucumber.
>
> Not feeling a meal one day? Swap it with another day's meal. That's allowed. Remember, no perfect days.

### 6. Your bonuses (7:45–8:45)

> You'll see two bonuses at the bottom of page 1.
>
> **Bonus one, the gut boost:** a forkful of kimchi or sauerkraut with lunch and dinner. Fermented foods bring friendly bacteria into your gut, and the fiber you're eating this week feeds them. If you're new to ferments, start with one forkful a day. Buy raw sauerkraut from the refrigerated section, not the shelf-stable kind.
>
> **Bonus two, for your nervous system:** your 60-second pre-meal audio. It's right inside this module. Press play before you eat. A body that feels safe can digest. It takes one minute and it changes the whole meal.

### 7. Grocery list + prep once (8:45–10:00)

> Turn to page 2. This is your grocery checklist. It's everything you need for these 7 days, and nothing you don't. Check it off as you shop.
>
> Then look at "Prep Once, Eat All Week":
> - On Day 1, make a big pot of lentil-leek soup. That's lunch on Days 1 and 3.
> - When you bake salmon on Days 1 and 4, make an extra fillet for the next day's salad. Cook extra chicken on Day 6 for Day 7.
> - The night before, boil your eggs, wash your greens and soak your overnight oats.
> - And fill your water bottle every night so it's waiting for you in the morning.
>
> Short on time? Canned lentils and chickpeas, pre-washed greens and frozen berries are completely fine.
>
> In the Bonus Swaps box you'll find options if you prefer, like sardines instead of salmon, beef instead of turkey, or tofu and tempeh for any protein.

### 8. Track your wins + what to expect (10:00–11:15)

> At the bottom of page 2 there's your tracker: Protein Win, Fiber Win, Water Win, for every day. Check the box when you hit it. You don't need all three every day. Most days, even imperfectly, is a win.
>
> Here's what many women notice in this first week:
> - More steady energy between meals.
> - Feeling fuller for longer.
> - Cravings starting to shift.
> - Some digestive changes as your gut adjusts. That usually settles quickly.
>
> And each morning, ask yourself one question: *How does my body respond when I nourish it early in the day?* Notice your energy, your hunger before lunch, your mood. No judgment. It's just information. Your body is talking to you.

### 9. Close + next step (11:15–12:00)

> Your only job this week is to nourish. Fuel before fine-tuning. Every meal is a chance to build trust with your body.
>
> So here's your first step: print your Meal Map, go shopping, and come post your **Day 1 win** in The Feminine Gut community. Tell me which win you hit first.
>
> Your body already knows what to do. We're just giving it what it needs.
>
> The future is yours. I'll see you inside.
