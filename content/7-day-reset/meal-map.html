This week you add, you don't subtract. Your body responds to abundance with wisdom and to scarcity with panic.

Your 3 Daily Wins

1

Protein Win

20–30 g per meal

Protein is your strongest natural GLP-1 trigger. Have it at breakfast within 2 hours of waking, not just at dinner.

2

Fiber Win

Soluble fiber every meal

Fiber feeds Akkermansia and the gut bacteria that make GLP-1: berries, apples, avocado, oats, flax, lentils, onion, garlic, leeks. New to fiber? Go slowly.

3

Water Win

½ body weight in oz

Start with 16–20 oz on waking (150 lb = 75 oz a day). Drink about 30 minutes before meals; ease off 2–3 hours before bed.

Prep Once, Eat All Week

  • Day 1: a big pot of lentil-leek soup (lunch Days 1 & 3).
  • Cook extra salmon on Days 1 & 4 for the next day's salad; extra chicken on Day 6 for Day 7.

  • Fill your water bottle each night.

Optional, if you like
