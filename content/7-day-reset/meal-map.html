Akkermansia & Natural GLP-1 foods that are skin & hormone friendly. Part of The 7-Day Feminine GLP-1 & Nervous System Reset.

The 3 Golden Rules

1

Protein & Fiber First

Start every meal with 25–30 g of protein and veggies before any starch or sweets. This wakes up your natural fullness signals (GLP-1).

2

Feed Your Akkermansia Daily

Colorful polyphenols (berries, pomegranate, green tea, cacao) + prebiotic fiber (onion, garlic, leeks, asparagus, green banana, oats) every day, plus a forkful of probiotic-rich fermented food (kimchi, sauerkraut, kefir) to bring in friendly bacteria.

3

Calm Before You Chew

Play your 60-second pre-meal audio, sit down, no screens, and chew slowly. A body that feels safe digests, balances hormones and lets go.

Build Your Plate

Breakfast

  • Yogurt bowl with berries, ground flax & chia
  • Veggie omelet with avocado & sautéed spinach
  • Overnight oats with pomegranate, walnuts & protein powder

Lunch

  • Salmon or chicken salad with arugula, olive oil & lemon
  • Quinoa bowl with roasted broccoli, chickpeas & tahini
  • Lentil-leek soup with a side of greens

Dinner

  • Wild salmon with asparagus & sweet potato
  • Beef or turkey stir-fry with bok choy, garlic & onion
  • Chicken thighs with Brussels sprouts & cauliflower mash

Snacks

  • Kefir or coconut kefir smoothie with berries
  • Green apple + almond butter
  • Berries & pumpkin seeds
  • Boiled eggs + cucumber
  • 2 squares 85% dark chocolate + green tea
