## Script

### 1. Celebrate the week (0:00–1:00)

> You made it through your first seven days. I want you to stop for a moment and really take that in.
>
> Look at your tracker. Every checked box was a moment you chose to nourish your body instead of restricting it. Every protein breakfast, every glass of water, every forkful of fiber was you building trust with your body.
>
> This was your foundation week. And a foundation is meant to be built on.

### 2. Check in: how did your body respond? (1:00–2:00)

> Before we talk about what's next, I want you to be honest with yourself about one question: *How did your body respond to these foods?*
>
> Maybe you noticed steadier energy, fewer cravings, feeling full longer, better digestion, maybe even better sleep. If that's you, your body is telling you: *"Yes, more of this."*
>
> Or maybe some of these foods didn't feel good. Maybe you got bloated after the yogurt or the kefir, or puffy after the eggs, or headaches, itching or a flushed face after the kimchi, or your stomach was upset with the lentils and chickpeas, even when you went slowly.
>
> Both answers are good information. Your body is talking to you. And the answer tells us which path is right for you next.

### 3. Path 1: Your body loved this → the full month (2:00–3:45)

> If your body felt good this week and you're not sensitive to any of these foods, this plan is incredible for you, and this is only the beginning.
>
> This week was Week 1 of a full four-week plan. We focused on just one pillar: nourishing first, with protein, fiber and water. But natural GLP-1 rests on four pillars: food, movement, sleep and stress.
>
> In **[FULL MONTH PROGRAM NAME]**, we keep building:
> - **Week 2: Slow and steady wins hormones.** Mindful eating, the timing of your meals, and small daily micro-habits that help your hormones find their rhythm, including your sleep.
> - **Weeks 3 and 4:** we bring in the movement that amplifies GLP-1 and the nervous system and stress practices that protect it, so your body can finally switch out of survival mode for good.
>
> Each week builds on the one before. You keep your Meal Map and your 3 Daily Wins, and we add one layer at a time, gently, so it actually lasts.
>
> Most women notice changes in the first week. The deeper changes, like reliable hunger and fullness cues, less bloating and easier weight balance, usually come in weeks two to four. That's what the full month is for.
>
> **[OPTIONAL, IF OFFERED: Because you finished the 7-Day Reset, you get [DISCOUNT/CREDIT] toward the full month.]**
>
> Tap the button below this video, **"Continue to the Full Month,"** to keep going.

### 4. Path 2: Food sensitivities → the hair test first (3:45–6:00)

> Now, if you're someone who reacts to foods, I want to speak directly to you.
>
> If you have food sensitivities, or this week some of these healthy foods made you feel worse, that is not you doing it wrong. And it's not a reason to start cutting out more and more foods and eating less and less. That's the restriction cycle we're trying to get you out of.
>
> When your body reacts to foods that should nourish it, it's usually a sign that something underneath needs attention first: your minerals, your stress load, your gut, or what your body has been exposed to. If we skip that step, we're just guessing. And guessing is what keeps so many women stuck.
>
> That's why, for you, the next step is **the hair test, inside The Hair Temple Blueprint.**
>
> A hair test shows us your mineral patterns and heavy metal exposure over roughly the last two to three months. It gives us a picture of how your body has been handling stress, blood sugar, your thyroid and adrenal patterns, and your hormones, which is why it helps explain why your body may be reacting the way it is.
>
> To be clear, it's not an allergy test and it doesn't diagnose anything. What it does is show us what your body has been going through, so instead of guessing, we build a plan made for *you*.
>
> Here's how it works:
> - You collect a small hair sample at home, and I'll walk you through exactly how.
> - Your results come back, and you get a personalized supplement plan built from them.
> - And I teach you how to read your own results, so you understand your body's story.
>
> Then, once your body is supported, we can bring these foods back in, and the four-week plan can work the way it's meant to.
>
> **[OPTIONAL, IF OFFERED: As a 7-Day Reset member, you get [DISCOUNT/CREDIT] on your hair test.]**
>
> Tap the button below, **"Get My Hair Test,"** to start.
>
> And of course, if you ever have a severe reaction to a food, like hives, swelling or trouble breathing, please see your doctor right away.

### 5. Not sure which? (6:00–6:45)

> Not sure which path is yours? Ask yourself two questions:
>
> 1. Did most of these foods feel good in my body this week?
> 2. Do I already know I'm sensitive to foods like dairy, eggs, legumes or fermented foods?
>
> If the first answer is yes and the second is no, the full month is your next step.
>
> If some foods didn't feel good, or you already know you're sensitive, start with the hair test.
>
> And if you're still not sure, post in the community and ask me. That's what we're there for.

### 6. Close (6:45–7:30)

> Whichever path you choose, remember this: your body was never the problem. It was waiting to feel nourished and safe. You gave it that this week, and it responded.
>
> So let's keep going. Choose your next step below, and I'll see you there.
>
> The future is yours.
