## 2. Audio: Pre-Meal 60-Second Vagal & Somatic Ritual

Record in Voice Memos in a quiet room, phone 6-8 inches away, slow and soft voice.
Pause where it says (pause). About 75-90 seconds.

### Script

> Before you eat, let's bring your body home. (pause)
>
> Sit down, place both feet on the floor, and let your shoulders drop away from your ears. (pause)
>
> Place one hand on your heart and the other on your belly. (pause)
>
> Breathe in through your nose for four... two, three, four. (pause)
> And breathe out slowly through your mouth for six... two, three, four, five, six. (pause)
>
> Again. In for four... and out, long and slow, for six. (pause)
>
> One more time. In... and let it all go. (pause)
>
> Now hum gently with your lips closed: "Mmmmmm." Feel the vibration in your chest and throat. That's your vagus nerve telling your body: you are safe. (hum for 5 seconds)
>
> Look at your food. Notice the colors, the smell. Let your mouth begin to water. (pause)
>
> Silently say to yourself: "My body is safe. My body is nourished. My body knows what to do." (pause)
>
> Now take your first bite slowly, and chew like you have all the time in the world. Because you do.
>
> Enjoy your meal, beautiful.
