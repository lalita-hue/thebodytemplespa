## Module 3: How to Read Your Hair Test

**Description (member-facing):**
> This is where your blueprint comes to life. Together, we'll open up a real report and I'll show you how to read it, section by section. You'll learn what each key mineral means for your body, why a "high" number isn't always too much, and how the ratios between minerals reveal your stress level, thyroid and adrenal patterns, blood sugar balance and hormone health. You'll discover your metabolic pattern, understand what toxic metals are showing up and why, and practice with sample reports until reading your own feels natural. By the end, your results won't look like a page of numbers anymore. They'll tell your body's story.

**Short version (card/thumbnail):**
> Learn to read your own results: the minerals, the ratios and the patterns that tell your body's story.

*The core teaching module. Walk through a sample report on screen.*

### 3.1 The layout of your report
- Nutrient minerals vs. toxic metals; the reference ranges; what "high" and "low" mean here.
- Why a high reading isn't always "too much" (minerals can be leaving tissue).

### 3.2 The main minerals
- Calcium & Magnesium: stress, muscles, sleep, blood sugar.
- Sodium & Potassium: adrenals, energy, stress response.
- Phosphorus: protein use and energy.
- Zinc & Copper: hormones, immunity, skin, mood.
- Iron, Manganese, Chromium, Selenium: energy, blood sugar, thyroid support.

### 3.3 The ratios (where the real insight is)
- Ca/Mg: blood sugar and stress.
- Na/K: adrenal stress and energy ("tired but wired").
- Ca/K: thyroid pattern.
- Na/Mg: adrenal pattern.
- Zn/Cu: hormones, estrogen and mood.
- Ca/P: how well you're using protein and burning energy.

### 3.4 Your pattern
- Fast vs. slow metabolism patterns, in plain language.
- Stress stage: where your body is in the stress response.
- Common patterns we see in women: burnout, "copper overload", blood sugar swings, low thyroid pattern.

### 3.5 Toxic metals
- Mercury, lead, cadmium, aluminum, arsenic: common everyday sources.
- Why they show up and why numbers can rise during the program (the body releasing them).

### 3.6 Practice
- 3 sample reports: members read them, then watch Lalita's answers.
- Worksheet: "My top 3 findings."
