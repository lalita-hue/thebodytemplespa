About This Guide

What it is & who it's for.

Manual Lymphatic Drainage (MLD) is a light, rhythmic technique that clears the fluid your body can't drain on its own — supporting recovery, reducing swelling, and helping you feel lighter, often within your first session.

Best fits for MLD

Post-surgery recoveryBBL, liposuction, tummy tuck, breast surgery, joint replacement — any procedure with lingering fluid retention.
LymphedemaPrimary or secondary; we work alongside your physician with Complete Decongestive Therapy protocols.
Inflammation & retentionThat persistent puffiness, heaviness, or bloating that just won't quit.
Pre & post-natalGentle support for swelling, circulation, and recovery.
Injury & sports recoveryAccelerated healing without aggressive deep-tissue work.

MLD is generally safe but contraindicated for active infection, blood clots (DVT), congestive heart failure, and some active cancers. We do a full health intake before every session.

Your First Session

What to expect.

No phone tag, no surprises. Here's exactly how it works — from the moment you reach out to the day you leave the table feeling lighter.

01

Intake & matching

Tell us what you're dealing with — post-op, lymphedema, chronic inflammation, pre/post-natal — and we match you to the right session length, pressure, and therapist. We text pricing & the next 3 open slots within the hour.

02

Health review & pre-care

Before your first session we do a full health intake (conditions, medications, recent surgeries). Pre-care: hydrate well, skip heavy meals 2 hours prior, wear loose comfortable clothing.

03

The session

Light, rhythmic pressure that opens the lymphatic system. Unlike deep tissue, it shouldn't be painful or cause soreness. Most clients feel a measurable drop in swelling before they leave the table.

04

Aftercare & next steps

A written aftercare plan goes home with you: hydration, no alcohol for 24 hours, gentle walking. After session one, your therapist gives a personalized recommendation for how many sessions to plan.
