Coach Report

Section scores organise what the client reported about their own symptoms. The bands are the same four the client sees on their own report, so you are never reading a different word for the same number. They are original coaching ranges — not validated medical cutoffs, not a diagnosis, and not a laboratory finding. N/A and blank answers are missing values and are never scored as zero. A section under 80% answered is left uncalculated rather than estimated. There is deliberately no combined health score. Do not show this page or these labels to a client.
