Coach Report

Section scores organise what the client reported about their own symptoms. They are original internal coaching ranges — not validated medical cutoffs, not a diagnosis, and not a laboratory finding. N/A and blank answers are missing values and are never scored as zero. A section under 80% answered is left uncalculated rather than estimated. There is deliberately no combined health score.
