FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What people ask before booking a first visit.

What actually happens at the first visit?+
We go through your full health history and your habits — how you sleep, what your energy does through the day, what your digestion is like, what has changed and when, and everything you have already tried. Then we give you an honest read on what is likely driving it, and a plan to start from. No testing on the day. Nothing sold to you on the table.
Do you offer GLP-1 support?+
Yes, as part of coaching rather than as a product on its own. We do not use the brand names you have seen advertised. Whether it is the right route for you is something we go through at your first visit, and sometimes the honest answer is no. What we will say is that it works better alongside everything else here: appetite is one lever, and if your gut, your minerals and your blood sugar are not addressed, you are pulling that lever without changing what sits underneath it. That is also why we do not hand it over and send you away.
Is this a diet?+
No. We do not hand you a meal plan, we do not weigh you every week, and there is no list of forbidden foods. What we do is work out what is actually going on with your metabolism, your gut and your hormones, then change the few things that matter most. If you have done the diets, you already know the problem was never information.
What testing do you run?+
Two, and only if the pattern calls for them. The GI-MAP is a microbial stool test — it measures the bacteria, yeast, parasites and pathogens living in your gut and how much of each is there, plus markers for how well you are digesting and how inflamed the gut lining is. The hair sensitivity screen tests a hair sample against 1,771 items rated high, moderate or no reactivity, covering foods, additives, metals and cosmetics, with readings on minerals, vitamins, hormone balance, digestion, sleep and stress. The hair screen is a wellness screen rather than a medical test — it is not an allergy test and it does not diagnose anything. We go through both reports with you line by line and you keep them.
Do I have to do testing?+
No, and not everyone needs it. The first visit is history and habits, and for some people what comes out of that conversation is enough to work with. Testing is for when the pattern keeps repeating and you want to know why rather than keep guessing. It is quoted separately so you are never paying for a panel you did not need.
How soon will I notice anything?+
We will not give you a number or a timeline, because anyone who does is guessing. What we can tell you is the order things usually change in: energy and digestion tend to shift first, sleep after that, and the things you can see take longest. If someone is promising you a specific result by a specific date, they are selling rather than coaching.
Do I have to give up coffee, bread or wine?+
Not as a rule, and not on day one. If testing shows you are reacting to something specific, we will usually suggest taking it out for six to eight weeks to see what changes — and then putting it back to find out whether it mattered. That is very different from a permanent list of things you are not allowed. A plan you resent is a plan you will stop.
I worked with a nutritionist and it didn't stick.+
Most people we see have. Usually what happened is that the advice was good in general but not built for what their body was actually doing — and when general advice does not work, it feels like a personal failure rather than a mismatch. Testing changes that conversation, because you are no longer arguing about willpower.
Is this instead of seeing my doctor?+
No. We work alongside your physician, not around them. If you are on medication, keep taking it and tell us what it is. If something we see needs medical attention, we will say so and tell you to go. Coaching is not medical care and we do not diagnose or treat disease.
How soon will you contact me after I submit?+
We typically reply within the hour during business hours and confirm all appointments within 24 hours by phone or text. For same-day availability, call or text us directly at (959) 400-9242 and we'll do everything we can to fit you in.
