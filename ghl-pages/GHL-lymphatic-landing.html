About This Guide

What it is & who it's for.

Manual Lymphatic Drainage (MLD) is a light, rhythmic technique that clears the fluid your body cannot drain on its own. It supports recovery, reduces swelling, and helps you feel lighter, often within your first session.

Common Questions

Before you book.

Does lymphatic drainage hurt?+
No. It is light, rhythmic and slow. Unlike deep tissue, it should never be painful and it should not leave you sore. Most people find it deeply relaxing and many fall asleep.
How many sessions will I need?+
It depends on why you are here. Post-surgery clients are usually recommended 6 to 12 sessions in the first weeks. Wellness and bloating clients often do well with one session monthly. Lymphedema is ongoing. Your therapist gives you a specific recommendation after session one, once we have seen how your body responds.
How soon after surgery can I start?+
Most surgeons clear patients between 3 and 7 days post-op, though some want you in sooner. Follow your surgeon's timeline and bring their instructions to your first visit. We work alongside your surgical team, not around them.
Will I see a difference after one session?+
Most clients see a measurable reduction in swelling before they leave the table, and many notice their clothes fitting differently the same day. How long it holds depends on hydration, movement and what is causing the retention in the first place.
What is the difference between lymphatic drainage and a regular massage?+
A regular massage works muscle with firm pressure. Lymphatic drainage works the lymphatic vessels just under the skin with very light pressure, in a specific sequence. Pressing harder does not drain more. It closes the pathway.
Is it covered by insurance or FSA/HSA?+
Lymphatic drainage for medically necessary care is often FSA and HSA eligible. Insurance coverage varies by plan and usually requires a physician referral. Ask us at intake and we will tell you what documentation we can provide.
Who should not get lymphatic drainage?+
It is not appropriate during active infection, with blood clots or DVT, with congestive heart failure, or with some active cancers. We do a full health intake before every session, and we will tell you honestly if this is not right for you.
What should I do before and after my session?+
Before: hydrate well, skip heavy meals for 2 hours, wear loose clothing. After: keep drinking water, skip alcohol for 24 hours, and walk gently. You go home with a written aftercare plan.
