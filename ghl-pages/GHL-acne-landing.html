FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What people ask before booking acne treatment.

Is this instead of seeing a dermatologist?+
No. We work alongside your dermatologist, not around them. If you are on a prescription that is helping, keep taking it and tell us what it is. What we add is the part most acne care skips — looking at what is driving the breakouts underneath the skin, so you are not dependent on the prescription forever.
How long before I see a difference?+
Your skin turns over in roughly 28 days, so one full cycle is the honest minimum before judging anything. Most people notice less inflammation and fewer new breakouts within the first few weeks, and see marks fade more slowly over two to three months. Anyone promising clear skin in a week is selling you something.
What testing do you actually run?+
Two panels. The GI-MAP is a microbial stool test. It measures the bacteria, yeast, parasites and pathogens living in your gut and how much of each is there, along with markers for how well you are digesting food and how inflamed the gut lining is. The hair sensitivity screen tests a hair sample against 1,771 items and rates each one high, moderate or no reactivity. It covers foods and drinks, additives, metals, cosmetics and fragrances, and also reports on minerals, vitamins, hormone balance, gut health, digestion, sleep, stress and skin. It is a wellness screen rather than a medical test — it is not an allergy test and it does not diagnose anything. What it gives us is a starting map, and the items it flags are usually worth removing for six to eight weeks to see what your skin does. We go through both reports with you line by line, and you keep them.
Do I have to do testing?+
No. Treating the skin on its own is a complete option, and plenty of people start there and do well. Testing is part of the combined program, and even then it comes after your first appointment rather than on the day. It is for when the pattern keeps repeating and you want to know why instead of guessing.
Will it make me break out first?+
It can, and we would rather you hear that from us than find out in the mirror. Bringing congestion to the surface is part of how it works, and some people get a short purge in the first week or two. We tell you what to expect for your skin before we start, and we adjust the strength if you are reactive.
I already cut out dairy and sugar and it didn't help.+
That is the most common thing we hear, and it is usually a sign the trigger was somewhere else. Elimination diets are a guess — they work when you happen to guess right. Testing tells you what your gut and your minerals are actually doing, so the changes you make are the ones that matter to your body rather than the ones that worked for someone on the internet.
Is there ever a reason to wait?+
Some treatments, chemical exfoliation among them, are not appropriate if you are pregnant or nursing, currently taking isotretinoin or within six months of stopping it, have an active cold sore or skin infection, or have had significant sun exposure in the last week. That does not mean we can do nothing, it means we choose differently. We do a full skin and health intake before every treatment and we will tell you honestly if the timing is wrong.
Is this for teenagers too?+
Yes, with a parent or guardian present for the first appointment. Teenage skin is often genuinely hormonal and settles with time, so we are careful not to over-treat it. If a gentler approach is the right call, we will say so rather than start a program.
