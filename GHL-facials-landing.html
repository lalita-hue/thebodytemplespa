FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to the questions clients ask before booking a facial.

Which facial should I start with?+
If it's your first visit, start with The Reveal Custom Facial (60 min). It includes advanced skin analysis and a full consultation, so you find out what your skin actually needs before spending money on a more advanced treatment. Everything else on the menu builds from what we learn there.
How often should I get a facial?+
Skin renews roughly every 4–6 weeks, so most clients maintain on that cycle. If you're actively correcting something — acne, texture, pigmentation — a shorter series of more frequent visits works better up front, then you space out. Your esthetician gives you a specific cadence after your analysis rather than a generic membership pitch.
Is there downtime after a peel or dermaplaning?+
Dermaplaning has essentially no downtime — skin looks smoother and takes makeup better right away. Chemical peels vary: light peels may cause mild flaking for a few days, while stronger formulas involve more visible peeling. We walk you through expected downtime before you book so you can plan around events, and we'll never go stronger than your skin is ready for.
What is microchanneling, and does it hurt?+
Microchanneling creates controlled micro-channels in the skin that stimulate collagen production and dramatically improve how well serums absorb. Most clients describe it as a light prickling sensation rather than pain, and expect some redness afterward — similar to a mild sunburn — that typically settles within 24–48 hours.
Can you treat active acne?+
Yes. Our acne treatment combines deep cleansing, extractions where appropriate, and calming therapy to reduce inflammation, and you leave with a home-care routine. Acne responds to consistency, so we'll be honest with you about how many sessions it's likely to take. If your skin needs prescription care, we'll tell you that too.
I have sensitive or reactive skin — is a facial safe?+
Usually, yes — and this is exactly why we analyze first. The Ultra Sonic Skin Scrubber, for example, deep-cleans without harsh extractions or irritation, and the lymphatic facials are extremely gentle. Tell us about your sensitivities, any active conditions (rosacea, eczema, cold sores) and any medications like Accutane or prescription retinoids at intake, and we'll adjust or reschedule accordingly.
What should I do before and after my facial?+
Before: come with clean skin if you can, skip exfoliating acids and retinoids for 3–5 days, and avoid waxing the area for at least a week. After: keep it simple — gentle cleanser, moisturizer, and daily SPF, which matters most after peels, dermaplaning and microchanneling. Your esthetician gives you written aftercare specific to your treatment.

We typically reply within the hour during business hours and confirm all appointments within 24 hours by phone or text. For same-day availability, call or text us directly at (959) 400-9242 and we'll do everything we can to fit you in.
