The Longevity Approach

Designed for the skin you will still have in ten years.

Repeatedly removed hair grows back finer and slower, and eventually stops — exactly what you want on legs, underarms and a bikini line. On a brow it is the opposite. So we treat them very differently.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

The things people ask before their first appointment.

How long does the hair need to be?+
About two to three weeks of growth, roughly a quarter of an inch — about the size of a grain of rice. Wax needs something to grip — come in too soon and it will miss hair, leaving you patchy and back within days. Resist tweezing in the week before your appointment, however tempting; those are exactly the hairs we need in order to see and correct your shape.
What happens at a first Brazilian?+
You are in a private room with the door closed and one esthetician, and you are talked through each step before it happens. You will be asked to undress from the waist down and given a towel; positioning is directed as we go, so you never have to guess. It takes about half an hour, the sharpest part is over in a few seconds at a time, and we stop whenever you ask. Nearly everyone says afterwards that the anticipation was worse than the appointment.
Does it hurt?+
There is a sharp second, and then it is over — most people find brows easier than they expected, and the upper lip and bikini line the most sensitive areas we treat. Hard wax is noticeably gentler than strip wax everywhere, which is why we use it. Skin is more sensitive in the few days before your period, so if you can, book outside that window.
Will my skin be red afterwards?+
Some pinkness is normal and usually settles within an hour or two — we finish with a calming balm before you leave to speed that along. If you are going somewhere that evening, book earlier in the day. Avoid heat, heavy sweating, sun and active skincare acids for the first twenty-four hours, since freshly waxed skin is more reactive than usual.
How often should I come back?+
Every three to four weeks for brows keeps the shape rather than rebuilding it, most people settle into four weeks for the lip and chin, and four to six weeks suits most body areas. Staying on a rhythm matters more than it sounds: hair regrows finer and sparser when it is removed consistently, and a maintained brow needs far less taken off each visit.
Which body areas do you wax?+
Underarms, arms, legs, back, chest, bikini and Brazilian. Body pricing depends on the area and how much is being treated, so rather than a fixed list we quote you when you tell us what you are after — use any of the pricing buttons on this page and we will come back to you, usually within the hour. Hard wax is used on the sensitive areas for the same reason we use it on the face.
Can you wax me if I use retinol or acids?+
Not on the treated area. Retinol, tretinoin, glycolic and salicylic acids all thin the surface layer of skin, and waxing over them can lift skin rather than just hair. Stop using them on the face for five to seven days beforehand. If you have taken Accutane within the last twelve months we cannot wax you at all — tell us at booking so we can suggest something else.
I am growing my brows out. Should I still come in?+
Yes, and this is one of the best reasons to see someone rather than doing it yourself. Growing out does not mean doing nothing — it means knowing which strays to remove so the brow still looks intentional while the shape fills in. Tell us that is what you are doing and we will take off far less than a standard wax, and tell you when to come back.
Why do I get bumps a few days later?+
Usually ingrown hairs, where regrowth cannot break the surface. It is far more common on the lip and chin than on brows. Gentle exfoliation two or three times a week from about day three keeps the path clear, and avoiding heavy occlusive products over the area helps. Consistent waxing reduces it over time; shaving in between makes it worse.
Can I wax over permanent makeup?+
Yes, once it has fully healed — but not during the healing period, and never over the pigmented area in the weeks right after a procedure. If you have had brow permanent makeup done with us or elsewhere, tell us when, and we will wax around the work rather than over it. Freshly tattooed skin needs to be left alone.
